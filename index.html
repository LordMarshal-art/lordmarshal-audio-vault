How do beat licenses and downloads work?

Beats are licensed through LordMarshal's official BeatStars store. Digital catalogue checkout and secure delivery will be activated after server-side payment verification is connected.

Which DAWs work with 485 MUSIC sample packs?

Standard WAV audio and MIDI files can be used in FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Cubase and many other music production applications. Check each product listing for its exact contents.

Are sample packs royalty-free?

Usage rights depend on the license supplied with each product. Read the product terms and included README before using sounds commercially.

When can other producers sell on 485 MUSIC?

Multi-vendor registration and producer dashboards are planned for Layer 2 after the initial catalogue launch.
