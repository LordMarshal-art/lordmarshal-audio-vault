Frequently asked questions.

Can I purchase products now?

The storefront is being prepared. Payment processing and automatic digital delivery have not yet been activated.

Can independent producers sell here?

Yes. Multi-vendor selling is part of the platform plan. Producer registration and approval still need to be implemented.

How will downloads work?

Customers will receive secure access to purchased files after the payment has been verified by the backend.

Are the products royalty-free?

Licensing will be specified individually for every product. Always check the licence before using a sound commercially.
