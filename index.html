Which DAWs can use these sounds?

Standard WAV audio and MIDI files work with most modern DAWs, including FL Studio, Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase and Studio One. Individual products may have additional requirements.

Are the sounds royalty-free?

Licensing depends on the source material and the license supplied with each product. Publish clear license terms for every pack before selling or distributing it.
