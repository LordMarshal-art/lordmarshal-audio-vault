Frequently Asked Questions

How do beat licenses and instant downloads work on 485 MUSIC?

Select any beat on 485 MUSIC, pick your preferred license tier (MP3 Lease, WAV Lease, Trackout Lease, or Exclusive Rights), and complete checkout. Once payment is verified, your private high-resolution files unlock immediately.

Which DAWs work with 485 MUSIC sample packs and MIDI kits?

All WAV audio files and MIDI collections are universally compatible with FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Cubase, Studio One, and MPC hardware.

What happens when someone buys Exclusive Rights to a beat?

Exclusive Rights grant unlimited commercial distribution, streaming, and ownership transfer per the beat agreement, and the beat is permanently retired from the 485 MUSIC storefront.
