Useful details

Frequently asked questions.

Can I purchase products now?

Select sounds can be added to your cart and checked out via Paystack once live keys are configured. Digital fulfillment and order receipts are verified after payment confirmation.

Can independent producers sell here?

Yes. Multi-vendor selling is part of the platform plan. Producer registration and approval will roll out after the core store.

How will downloads work?

Customers receive secure access to purchased files after payment has been verified by the backend.

Are the products royalty-free?

Licensing is specified individually for every product. Always check the licence details before commercial release.

Which DAWs can use these sounds?

Standard WAV audio and MIDI files work with most modern DAWs, including FL Studio, Logic Pro, Ableton Live and Cubase. Individual products may have additional requirements.
