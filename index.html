How do beat licenses and downloads work?

Beats in the Beat Store are licensed and delivered automatically through BeatStars upon checkout. Sample packs and vault kits in the Digital Catalogue checkout via Paystack and unlock secure digital download links after payment verification.

Which DAWs work with 485 MUSIC sample packs?

All drum loops, 808s, melodies, and kits are formatted in standard 24-bit/16-bit WAV and MIDI so they load straight into FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Cubase, and mobile beat-making apps.

Are sample packs royalty-free?

Each sample pack and drum kit includes clear usage terms on its product card and inside the downloaded archive's README license file.

When can other producers sell on 485 MUSIC?

485 MUSIC launches Layer 1 (LordMarshal's official catalogue) on November 30, 2026. Multi-vendor producer registration and dashboards roll out immediately after in Layer 2.
